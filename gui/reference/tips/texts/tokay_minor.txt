Tokay of Crescent Island (Minor Faction)
The lizard-like inhabitants of Crescent Island are thieves, bandits, and raiders. While not evil, they have an obsession with anything shiny or colorful, especially if from a foreign land.
The religon of the Tokay is largely unknown, even to them. They live around a temple in the shape of a Tokay head, which they call the Maku Temple. Perhaps this is a remnant of when Crescent Island was attached to Labrynna, hence the Maku Tree.
