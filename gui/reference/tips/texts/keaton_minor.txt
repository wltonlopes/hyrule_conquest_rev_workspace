Keaton Cartel (Minor Faction)
The Cartel is an organization dedicated to furthering their power and influence, as well as growing their already impressive stash of money and loot. The Cartel opperates much as a thieve's guild, building hidden dens and safehouses in every major city, as well as scattered throughout the wilderness.
While they prefer to stay out of conflicts, when needed they use an impressive network of spies, thugs, and assassins to deal with the enemies of their employer. They also excel at ambushes, hitting an occupiedenemy from behind and then fading into the wildernes before they can be hit.
