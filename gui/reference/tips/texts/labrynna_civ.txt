Labrynna Empire
Once a shining beacon of civilization in the east, Labrynna served as a Province to the Kingdom of Hyrule and important connection to the Great Sea. In recent years the witch Veran has managed to worm her way into the trust of the Labrynnian Royal Court, advancing their military might and political power. Now no longer a Province of the Kingdom of Hyrule, Labrynna plans to challenge the crown with its expanding armies.
Labrynna is ruled over by Queen Ambi, who delegates many of her tasks and buisness to a group of Lord Commanders called the Council. They in turn leave many decisions up to their many judges, brigada, and burgemeisters.
