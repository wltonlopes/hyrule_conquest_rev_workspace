The Great Deku Tree
This massive hulking being is the lord of the entire Kokiri Forest and the patron deity of the many woodland people. It is through his power that the Kokiri stay young and are safe from the evil wiles of the Baga Tree. During times of war, he uproots himself to defend his forest.
The Great Deku Tree is a powerful creature that can hold multiple Kokiri archers and slingshotters, while he stomps his way towards the enemy armies.
