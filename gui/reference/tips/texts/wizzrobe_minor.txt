Order of the Wizzrobe (Minor Faction)
The Order of the Wizzrobe is a highly secretive group, made up of bird-like magicians and wizards. They are feared across Hyrule for their deadly magical skills, which they sometimes sell to others.
The Wizzrobes are said to have once been a mighty civilization, at the hieght of magical knowledge and power. However, this same magic destroyed them in a civil war, with only a few hundred surviving today.
While the true purpose of the Order is unknown, they seem to have been granted their powers by the goddess Hylia.
