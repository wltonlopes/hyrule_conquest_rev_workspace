Darknut Legion (Minor Faction)
Once a great and mighty empire, the Darknut Legion was a paragon of military might and power. However, weakened by plots, uprisings, and rebellions from their unhappy serfs, the Legion has lost much of it's former glory, with most surviving members serving as mercenaries and bodyguards to the other actions of Hyrule.
Still, the ruler of the surviving Legion, Zalunbar, hopes that he can oneday rekindle the power of the Legion, sowing fear in the hearts of all who would dare stand in the way of their highly trained and heavily armored armies.
