Deku Kingdom
The Kingdom of the Deku Scrubs are a loose collection of feudal territories deep within the eastern reaches of the Kokiri Forest. Known for their irrational bursts of anger and their perpetual short-man's complex, large gatherings of these normally comical forest dwellers can result in terrifying destruction and chaos when unleashed on Hyrule.
For years, the Scrubs were to busy with civil wars and infighting to bother any other countries. However, since King Kud-Loh-Kan united them, they have shown much intrest in the lands of other countries.
