Lon Lon Ranch (Minor Faction)
Famous acroos Hyrule for their delicious Lon Lon Milk and amazing horses, Lon Lon Ranch is owned and run by Malon Lontha, who's skill at horseback archery is matched only by the Gerudo horse archers.
While there are dozens of Lon Lon locations scattered throughout Hyrule, the main one, Lon Lon Ranch, is located in Hyrule Field, making it a vassal of the Kingdom of Hyrule. This does not stop them from selling their horses to the other races of Hyrule however.
