Zuna (Minor Faction)
The Zuna are a race of lizard people who dwell in the Gerudo desert. They are merchants and arms dealers, and are renowned for their high quality siege equiment and honest trade practices.
They are rumored to have made alliances with the Darknut Legion while it was at it's peak. There are also tales of wars fought with the Zuna serving as mercenaries, selling their siege weapons to the highest bidder.
