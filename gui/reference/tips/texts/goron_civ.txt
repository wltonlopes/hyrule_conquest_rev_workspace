Gorons
Mighty rock-men hailing from the peaks of Death Mountain, the Gorons are a people of warriors, miners and boisterous party-goers. Hyrule's mightiest weapons are crafted by their hands, and the outcome of many wars has rested on their involvement. Their tenacity for battle is legendary and has earned them a place in the vast history of the land.
The society of the Gorons is patriarchal (due to the fact that they, apparently, don't seem to have any women at all). Each Goron tribe is lead by a Patriarch, but they are all ruled over by King Darunia, the Sage of Fire.
