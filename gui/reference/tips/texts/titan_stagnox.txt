Stagnox
This giant hulking Orhat Beetle has long lurked on the eastern forests of Hyrule. In some cases the Deku Scrubs have been known to coerce this huge monster into fighting on their behalf. The Stagnox may be slow, but his armor is some of the most impressive and durable in Hyrule. While the Stagnox has wings, his huge size makes his ability to fly very limited.
Stagnox is a powerful creature that tears into enemy armies with his massive horn, deflecting most attacks with his massive shell.
