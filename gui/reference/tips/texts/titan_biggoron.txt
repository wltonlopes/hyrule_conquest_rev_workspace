Biggoron
Perhaps the largest Goron in all of recorded history, Biggoron lives a mostly solitary life on the peak of Death Mountain. It is there that he spends most of his time perfecting the refinement of metals. His work has become the basis for forging some of the Goron's most powerful weaponry and armor. Despite his name being associated with weapons and bloodshed, Biggoron is a pacifist at heart, and creates his work for the sake of art and self-defense.
Towering above hs fellow Gorons, Biggoron is a terrifying sight to behold as he casually kicks over fortifications and swats aside armies, his thick skin blocking most arrows.
