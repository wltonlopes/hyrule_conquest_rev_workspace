Fairy Enclave
Perhaps one of Hyrule's greatest mysteries, Fairies are mythical and reclusive beings that, despite being found in all corners of the world, are rarely if ever seen. Most of the time the Fairies go about on their own with little unity or guidance, tending to nature or causing mischief when no one is looking. As the land cries out in pain however, these wild principles gather together and cause a lot more than a little mischief.
While each group of fairies is led by a Great Fairy, they are ruled over by Valfi, the Queen of all Fairies. Only the extremely foolhardy or brave would dare cross her, lest be frozen to their bones by the power of winter.
