Ordona Province
Established as a vassal state of the Kingdom of Hyrule, Ordona Province has grown to become the most powerful agrarian nation in all of history. What they lack in military might they make up for with a powerful economy fueled by crops, livestock, and some of the finest brew this side of Lake Hylia. Motivated by hard work and having a good time, the people of Ordona Province are nonetheless some of the toughest folks around.
The people of Ordona Provice are known to be tougher than most Hylians, a side effect of spending their entire lives farming and homesteading.
