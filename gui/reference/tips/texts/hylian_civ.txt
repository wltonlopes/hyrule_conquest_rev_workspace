Kingdom of Hyrule
The imperial monarchy known as the Kingdom of Hyrule is one of the longest-lived and most powerful empires to have existed. For nearly three-thousand years the Hylian people have rallied behind the banner of the winged Triforce, creating one of the largest and most successful armies the land has ever known.
Hyrule is split into 19 fiefs, with the capital being the 20th. While each fief swears fealty to the King and follows the same basic laws, they act semi-independently, creating their own images, acting in commerce, and becoming known for ther individual skills in certain crafts.
