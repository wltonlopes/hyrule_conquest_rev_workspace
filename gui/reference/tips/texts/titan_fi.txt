Fi
The spirit of the Master Sword, Fi was bound to the Blade of Evil's Bane by Hylia, shortly before Hylia gave up her immortaity to become a mortal for a time. Though she was bound unwillingly, Fi has come to enjoy the power that she has gained, and uses it in defense of Hyrule.
Fi is a powerful and fast spirit, able to float over water and hack enemies to pieces with spiritual swords.
