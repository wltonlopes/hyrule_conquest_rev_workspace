[font="sans-bold-16"] # Hyrule Conquest: Revival
[font="sans-16"] This mod is a revival of the original Hyrule Conquest mod made by The Undying Nephalim. It adds races, maps, and other features from the Legend of Zelda series.

[font="sans-bold-16"] ## What's new this version
[font="sans-16"]
 - Balancing & Bug Fixes
   - Mercenaries now cost Rupees and don't count towards max population
   - Most tehnologies cost Rupees
   - Fortresses, towers, and civil centers now spawn their garrison.
   - New Deku Kingdom unit.
   - Fixed the Legatus and Gleeok.
   - Some units now spawn units on death, mainly Zola and Fairy units.
   - Fixed the Shrapnel Flinger.
   
 - Civs
   - Added civ-specific battle standards.
   - Added technologies for Labrynna.
   - Reworked Labrynna's buildings.
   
 - Maps
   - Added new skirmish maps: Crucible of Hylia (2), Faron Woods (2), Plains of Ordona (6), Misery Mire (3), Mountains of Lanayru (3), and The Lost Woods (2)
   
 - Other
   - Added a new resource: Rupees
   - Added SpawnEntityOnDeath
   - Added SpawnEntityOnHit
   - Added SpawnEntityOnImpact

   - A new AI called Scrub Bot, that is designed only for the Deku Kingdom civ.
